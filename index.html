      Under the hood

      How it works

      Randomness comes from crypto.getRandomValues, the browser's cryptographic generator. Only the settings, the username and the list of terms are remembered, and only in this browser.

      Entropy is the number of bits of uncertainty, assuming the attacker knows the word lists and your settings. Each extra bit doubles the number of guesses needed to find the password.

      The weak password list contains the most common Polish and English passwords. The target system may have a longer one, so the generator avoids them with a margin. The rule about the previous 10 passwords is checked only by the system: every generated password is new.

      English words in the "Words" mode come from the EFF Large Wordlist (Electronic Frontier Foundation, licensed under CC BY 3.0 US).
